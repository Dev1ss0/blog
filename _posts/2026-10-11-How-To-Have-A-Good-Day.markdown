I feel like I am in a loop. Engaging myself in a bad habit, feel shame, feel disconnected from the "narrative of my life", numb the feelings with even more comfort substances, feel even more guilt and shame, and even less natural motivation. This process is called "self destruction". I am sure you have done this before. I know I have. But how to stop feeling like shit? How to avoid this toxic loop?

## It is time for a change.
See, I noticed something, the more "bad" stuff I do, the more shit my day is, and by "bad", I mean, procrastination, instant gratification, bad habits, scrolling, etc. I'm sure you get the point. But on the other side, the more good things I do, the better my day is. So now, how to fix this?

I am a firm believer that routine is the most important thing in a day, without it, I just don't feel like I am doing enough.. (ofc if I don't do anything else than the routine I feel that way too). Routine makes you do the basic stuff like, brushing your teeth, cleaning your room, making your bed, doing a quick workout etc. I know it sounds and looks easy, but making a routine even as simple as that genuinely helps build up a good positive momentum, which, at least for me is the driving force to do more good and productive stuff, without relying on bad habits like scrolling, and many other bad habits.

Of course it is not only about routine. I have not been doing things that I have said I would, that has made my brain not trust me as much as it did. Actually sticking with the plans that I've made or doing things that I've said heals that, it makes me feel like I am doing good, that everything will be fine. I don't know, maybe I am just broken...

## But why do we scroll instead of doing meaningful things?
Scrolling or really any other pleasure seeking addiction gives us instant gratification, it gives us comfort. We are living in a world where we have pleasure at our finger tips. The technology is just going to get better and better, it is designed to keep us hooked. We just have to stay human.

So.. Make a routine, do things that you say you will, find hobbies, things that actually interest you, create something that isn't in this world yet. The sky is the limit.
